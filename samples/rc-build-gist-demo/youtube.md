## Description

I built a radio receiver USB adapter for my RC sim-racing controller — from
the 3D-printed case to the Arduino firmware and the on-track test.

The case was designed in my own 3D editor, "3table". The firmware is a small
Arduino sketch that turns RC PWM signals into joystick axes. The track test
was hard to drive because I was recording the video with one hand and steering
with the other!
