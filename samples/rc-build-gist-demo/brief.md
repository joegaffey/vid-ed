# RC controller build — demo

A short, friendly demo for RC sim-racing hobbyists. It combines two videos:
first, building the radio receiver USB adapter case; then, the DIY controller
in use on track. Tone: warm, enthusiastic, first person.

Context:
- The case was designed in my own 3D editor, called "3table".
- The on-track test was hard to drive: I was recording the video with one hand
  and steering with the other. That made driving one-handed the real challenge.
